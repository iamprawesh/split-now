Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Split Now:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile picture from Google or Apple sign-in.
  • App Data: Groups, expenses, settlements, and other content you create within the App.
  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, and Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens for push notifications.
  • Crash Data: Anonymous crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix bugs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's functionality.
  • To send push notifications about group activity.
  • To improve the App through analytics and crash reporting.
  • To respond to your support requests.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config, and Crashlytics. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Other Users: Your name, avatar, and expense data are visible to members of groups you join.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

5. Your Rights

  • Access and update your profile information at any time.
  • Delete your account — contact us at nxn.shrestha@gmail.com.
  • Opt out of push notifications in device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Split Now is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS) and secure storage for tokens. However, no method of transmission is 100% secure.
